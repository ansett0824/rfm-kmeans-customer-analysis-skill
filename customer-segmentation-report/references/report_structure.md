# Customer Segmentation Report Structure

## Recommended Excel Worksheets

### 1. Report Overview

Purpose:

Provide a quick summary for managers.

Suggested contents:

- Analysis period
- Total number of customers
- Total sales amount
- Number of high-value customers
- Number of general customers
- Number of at-risk customers
- Sales contribution by customer segment
- Key findings
- Management recommendations

### 2. Customer RFM Summary

Purpose:

Show each customer's RFM calculation result.

Suggested columns:

- Customer name
- Last purchase date
- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary
- R score
- F score
- M score
- Total RFM score

### 3. Customer Segmentation Results

Purpose:

Show the K-means clustering result and final customer type.

Suggested columns:

- Customer name
- R score
- F score
- M score
- Cluster
- Customer segment
- Total sales amount
- Transaction frequency
- Suggested action

### 4. High-value Customer List

Purpose:

List customers that should be prioritized.

Suggested columns:

- Customer name
- Total sales amount
- Transaction frequency
- Last purchase date
- Main product
- Region
- Recommended follow-up action

### 5. At-risk Customer List

Purpose:

List customers that may need follow-up.

Suggested columns:

- Customer name
- Last purchase date
- Months since last purchase
- Past sales amount
- Product purchased
- Region
- Suggested reactivation action

### 6. Regional Sales Analysis

Purpose:

Analyze sales performance by region.

Suggested charts:

- Sales amount by region
- Number of customers by region
- Customer segment distribution by region

### 7. Product Sales Analysis

Purpose:

Analyze which products contribute more sales.

Suggested charts:

- Top products by sales amount
- Top products by quantity
- Product preference by customer segment

### 8. Charts and Dashboard

Purpose:

Place important visualizations in one dashboard sheet.

Suggested charts:

- Customer segment distribution
- Sales contribution by segment
- Top 10 customers
- Pareto chart
- RFM distribution
- K-means cluster scatter plot

### 9. Data Quality Notes

Purpose:

Record data problems and assumptions.

Suggested contents:

- Missing columns
- Missing customer names
- Negative sales records
- Duplicated records
- Date format issues
- Fields not available for analysis
